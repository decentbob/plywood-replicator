# Portfolio checkpoint after the delivery replay

2026-09-27, ROADMAP Q4; baseline `c710056`. Static comparison of two causal
contrasts, using RESULTS 47, 49–51, 61–62, 66 and 71–75 and the current
`Sim.compat` / `_compat0` implementation. No new measurements or simulation.

**Decision: neither contrast earns an assay now.** Chemical efficacy is the
cleaner intervention, but does not isolate recipient benefit from changes to
the producing population. The mechanical contrast is closer to inherited
function, but mainly joins two already demonstrated results: fit-dependent
copying and finite descendant renewal. Neither currently predicts a useful
organization outperforming its simpler competitor under the same fixed rules.
This is a decision about research value, not evidence that either effect is zero.

## A. Suppress chemical efficacy while retaining product attachment

**Question and prediction.** How much of the section-62 population output
depends on the programmed acceleration of lateral linking, given that products
can still form and attach? If acceleration matters at this level, the original
world should produce more detached exact output than the efficacy-off world.
This differs from `pBindP=0`, which removes physical attachment as well.

**Smallest intervention.** Keep the original 1,000-block inventory, production,
binding, melting, geometry, bare rate 0.01 and all other parameters. In a
research subclass only, return `pLinkBare` for every eligible letter-letter
STICKY lateral pair, including supported ones. Retain free product-product
linking and every other compatibility case. Compare ordinary rules, this
efficacy-off arm, and the existing no-binding competitor. No selective
recipient/producer intervention is permitted.

**Block contract and cost.** The changed compatibility case reads contacting
side states, immutable letter/product labels and the fixed bare probability;
ordinary contact geometry still gates an incident bond request. It adds no
state write, type conversion, relay, force or organism reader. Cost: one
research rule override and one assay arm selector; zero new mutable states,
side marks or core knobs. The ordinary arm retains its existing derived
`cat` reads and the audit's live-side caveat. Do not claim a new locality proof.
`catalysis=false` is not this ablation: `compat` also uses it to permit mature
product binding. `pLinkBare=1` is not this ablation either.

Matched initial arrays and RNG are feasible. Later product abundance,
occupation and encounters will diverge because both producer and recipient
linking can change. Identical binding *rules* do not preserve binding *history*.
A nonzero population contrast would establish a total effect of chemical
efficacy under these conditions, not a direct effect on a fixed recipient or
necessity of its particular observed supporter.

**Path to inherited function and missing link.** A physically transmitted
organization would need to produce or acquire useful support, retain the
benefit through descendant replacement, and do better than ordinary short
renewing rows. The proposed uniform ablation tests the supplied linking rule;
it does not hold the producer-mediated environment fixed or identify that
organization. Even a large total-birth difference would leave this gap. The
existing stock exact counts and sampled 5k integrity checks are not a substitute
for uninterrupted, reproducing descendant histories.

**Admission / cost / stop gates.** Before admitting a population plan, require
an identifiable inherited-organization endpoint whose contrast distinguishes
recipient function from loss of producer supply without IDs in reactions.
No such endpoint/intervention pair is supplied by the current evidence.
If one is proposed later, first require prepared probability, physical-binding,
restart and observer-neutrality checks: only the intended lateral probability
may differ at a fixed state. Failures stop design, not trigger core repairs.
For scale only, eight original-sized 50k worlds would cost roughly 1,791 run
CPU seconds at section 75's observed average, before fixtures/QA; this is not
a guarantee for changed trajectories. An admissible bounded proposal would
need a cost-only pilot, a separate QA reserve and an all-process total cap
(at most 2,400 CPU seconds for that size), with viability checked at 10k and
no extension to rescue sparse recipient output. No seeds, runner or batch are
queued by this comparison; the missing identification gate fails first.

**Disposition:** technically separable, scientifically insufficient for the
current priority. Retain as a possible calibration if a future functional
hypothesis needs it. Do not implement it just to repeat that the supplied
catalytic rule accelerates a permitted link.

## B. Test whether complementary mechanical fit is required for renewal

**Question and prediction.** Extend the section-49 pairing-mode contrast to
descendant renewal: opposed wedges should lose more renewal on switching
complementary to self recognition than square material does. This is a new
causal endpoint, unlike another comparison of the failed eight- or five-letter
arrangements, but the elementary fit effect is already established.

**Smallest intervention.** Existing `compCopy` on/off crossed with opposed20 /
square shapes, equal A/B material and initially active balanced founders in
the section-66 fuel/turnover environment. Same initial geometry within each
shape; fixed fuel, inventory, stiffness and turnover. Use mode-appropriate
physical parent/child mapping: self and complementary recognition generate
different sequences. Neither identical child strings nor later encounters can
be held fixed by this intervention. Square self recognition is the simplest
matched competitor; renewing square dimers from 71 are the separate simpler
organization that a claimed extra function ultimately must beat.

**Block contract and cost.** Face compatibility reads the contacting immutable
labels and exposed DOCK/template states, requests an incident bond and uses
the existing identity/complement table. Existing type-specific rest shapes
affect numerical deformation; actual corners and face angles must be measured.
Fuel arming and turnover stay unchanged. Zero new rules, mutable states,
side marks or knobs. No ancestry, row identity or completion reader enters
reactions. Keep body jostling for exploration; a promising mechanical claim
would need matched individual kicks with adequate solver resolution, as in P0.

**Path to inherited function and missing link.** Descendants can inherit the
letter order and thus its geometry; 66/72 already demonstrate finite physical
renewal. A useful result would be an inherited mechanical operation with a
reproductive advantage over the same-material simple competitor. The proposed
positive interaction instead says that a matching rule rescues otherwise
incompatible wedges. It supplies no reason why that rescued organization is
useful relative to square renewal. Changing the recognition table between
worlds is an experimental ablation, not innovation under fixed rules.

**Admission / cost / stop gates.** Require a named physical operation beyond
ordinary copying, a causal obstruction it removes, and a prediction of benefit
against square renewal before any viability run. Current evidence supplies
none: capture alone failed to imply benefit, and both arrangement screens
failed. Were that gate met by genuinely different evidence, require detached
exact founder output in a bounded 20k viability check, then an endpoint of
fuel-witnessed nonfounder reproduction before retirement, with unfinished,
shorter, variant and age-censored outcomes retained. Reject a proposal whose
only success criterion is more first-generation output. An eight-world 100k
comparison at section 72's screen cost is roughly 378 run CPU seconds; cap an
initial proposal including viability and QA at 900, with a cost pilot and
reserved QA. P0/fresh confirmation would need its own earned plan and budget.
These are admission ceilings, not a frozen assay or permission to run it.

**Disposition:** closer to the goal and cheaper, but low new discrimination.
Do not repeat the known fit rescue with a later endpoint unless an additional
inherited physical function has a causal benefit prediction.

## Decision and reopening condition

A ranks first for rule-level separation; B ranks first for mechanical goal fit,
simplicity and estimated cost. Both fail the inherited-function criterion.
Therefore **zero prospective assay plans and zero simulation steps are earned**.
No new empirical negative is claimed; the earlier failed gates remain intact.

The missing measurement is a **same-material causal benefit of an inherited
physical organization over simple renewal**, surviving descendant turnover.
For ecology that requires separating useful recipient action from a changed
producer environment; for mechanics it requires an operation that helps beyond
making a distorted template copyable. These are alternative entry conditions,
not requirements to solve both at once. A prepared contrast can establish the
operation first, but must name how its parts could be acquired and renewed
without observer intervention before it earns a population screen.

Next research should nominate that operation and its smallest counterfactual,
using an existing physical witness or a distinct local-mechanics hypothesis.
Require an explicit prediction for the simpler competitor. Do not automatically
start another census, replay, literature survey, random-table search or parameter
sweep. There is presently no admitted executable assay. The absence of a current
candidate is not a proof that useful organization is impossible.
