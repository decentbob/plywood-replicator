## What the ecology archive can and cannot answer

Source inspection of `recipient_dependence.js` shows that `sample` records
aggregate producer/recipient sites and mature occupancy every 100 steps.
`observe` records birth members and one sampled 5k follow-up. `run` saves
parameters, an initial physical hash, births and final state, but no ordered
product-contact or lateral-link tape. `maxEventLog` is zero. Thus the existing
archive cannot assign an individual delivery to a subsequent linking event,
or distinguish lost recipients from failed encounters. A lagged correlation
of aggregate occupancy and births would not resolve that gap.

An observation-only deterministic replay could collect the missing events,
but would cost simulation CPU and remains a retrospective diagnostic. Do not
launch it merely because the old aggregate effect was positive. Its design
must first demonstrate, on small fixtures, that each proposed obstruction has
a distinct observable signature and that logging preserves all physical
arrays, counters and RNG. A copy of the historically hashed runner may be
wrapped; its source and rules must remain untouched.

## Next bounded task: Q3 diagnostic design, before replay

Specify block-level event witnesses for (1) geometric product/recipient
contact opportunity, (2) a mature product actually binding an eligible
recipient side, (3) local lateral linking while that support is available,
and (4) complete detached physical output with continuous member history.
Use explicit unknown classes where these cannot be reconstructed. Track
recipient site availability so donor delivery is not blamed for absent
recipients. Distinguish transient binding from productive support; timing
alone is not a causal benefit.

Keep all IDs and classifications in observation. No new chemical states,
marks, types, forces or identity-aware ablations. Compare existing on/noBind/
noSource/neither arms; same-seed arms match initial conditions only. Before
any archived-world replay, freeze the worlds, observation horizon, CPU cap,
obstruction criteria and stop gate. Include both failed and successful
confirmation worlds rather than selecting the low-occupancy seed alone.
Validate a neutral fixture and show that the proposed metrics distinguish
the alternatives. If a matched causal follow-up cannot conserve material
and distinguish contact availability from product action, report that and
stop design. No parameter repair or new population batch follows by default.

This is the next discriminating measurement to design, not evidence that
delivery timing explains the failures. Its possible path to inherited
function is a productive material exchange among renewing assemblies; that
still requires causal benefit and persistence through descendant turnover.
