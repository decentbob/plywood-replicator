# Local assembly fronts (prospective, 2026-09-26)

Research question: can contact-gated recruitment make assembly advance along a
surface without a controller, a length counter, or a completion detector?
Constructor research separates information copying from useful construction;
signal-passing tiles suggest local activation as an assembly primitive. This is
an experimental primitive, not a universal constructor or an evolutionary claim.

First replay section 53's selected curved seed 83, undock 0.3, 50k. Count actual
engine docking and linking gates and per-step occupancy from 15k onward. Observe
the archived anchors 87 and 107 (observation only), and their next required sites
2 and 3 respectively. Reconcile births, material snapshots and full final state
against an uninstrumented replay. A site occupied by the other patch and an open
site rejecting incoming geometry must not be conflated.

Then test a research-only contact gate. An armed letter advertises on its bonded
lateral sides whether its own face is occupied. A free face is receptive only if
one designated lateral side is unbonded or its bonded partner advertised face
occupancy in the previous derive pass. Bound faces retain the ordinary side
state, so release/holding requirements do not change. Test both L and R polarity,
plus an ungated control. All arrays are derived per side; no rule reads IDs,
sequence, age, position in a row, component membership, or observational counts.
No core change. Preserve exact off trajectories and one-pass signal delay.

Use the section-53 setup: one PAAAABBBBQ, square or opposing 20-degree wedges,
150 conserved blocks, no energy or turnover. Screen new seeds 85/86, undock 0.1,
50k, four workers. Report exact births at 20k/50k, errors, nuclei, mergers and
unfinished material. The experimental gate is installed after seed setup; initialize
its derived side marks to zero and derive once, identically for both polarities.

Advance a polarity only if curved exact births at 50k improve in both seeds,
neither 20k output decreases, and no inexact births occur in either shape.
Tie-break: larger summed curved 50k output, then R. Confirm only the qualifying
polarity on fresh seeds 87-94, both shapes, 50k, control matched. Otherwise stop
and retain the negative result. No post-hoc parameter search to rescue a failure.
Any confirmed shape claim needs solver controls. A viable gate alone does not
show a constructed catalyst, sustained descendants, or increased complexity.

Keep raw events, parameters, hashes, complete grids, CPU costs and tests. Store
running outputs in experiments/scratch, copy finished outputs to experiments/out.
