100k runs, seeds 73-76, square/opposed20, with the exact same setup and random
streams. These are reused worlds for diagnosis, not independent confirmation.
Do not change engine rules, state, material or geometry during this assay.

Observe birth events to retain the actual member IDs of each released row and
its parent-member provenance. Observe individual rearming events and record
when each known row first has every site active. Sample every 100 steps: first
observed incoming monomer, maximum docked sites, and first observed joined
incoming pair. Record all per-row states and whole-world material partitions
every 10k. Distinguish founders, logged offspring, free monomers, isolated
docked monomers, and linked material not yet logged as a birth. Split that last
category into still-attached versus detached rows and record states and lengths;
do not label every unlogged unit a failed copy.

how many fully rearm and how many produce a logged child by 100k? Also retain
all later offspring with their shorter exposure explicitly. Attribute child
production by recorded parent member IDs, never by sequence alone. Full rearming
is a diagnostic milestone, not a programmed requirement for starting a partial
copy. Reconcile all 120 letters, all births and all fuel consumptions. Compare
ordinary birth logs and 10k stats exactly to the archived section-50 replay.

Test observation neutrality on complete saved states, including the RNG; test
material categories and event membership. Check actual lateral row integrity.

with same-seed controls. No new reward or mechanism is justified in advance.

already present at the fork. Track loss of each original row at its first fray
