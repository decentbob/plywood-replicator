Choose the **short-variant renewal census**. Its causal hypothesis concerns
retention of function through variation, not which prepared eight-letter
sequence wins. The measurement can reject a stock-birth interpretation or
identify a concrete lineage to investigate without designing another machine.
The other two candidates lack a presently isolated causal obstruction or a
working prerequisite. Handoff's four unproductive cycles (70) warn against
improving an operation without measuring useful descendants. Random-chemistry
controls (64–65) warn that recurrent strings alone are not heredity.

The [frozen census plan](short_variant_plan.md) specifies the entire archived
matrix, provenance requirements, controls and gate before new counts are
computed. It is a retrospective diagnostic on known worlds, not a prospective
fresh-seed confirmation. A positive census can nominate a candidate, not certify
heritable benefit, shape advantage, novelty or sustained selection. A negative
or unidentifiable census earns no automatic additional seeds or mechanisms.
