# Sequence-encoded shields under the damage field (within-world selection)

2026-09-29; ROADMAP 1. Frozen before running. Screen tier.

**Why.** Paired walled/bare worlds (RESULTS 103–104) favour walls in most seeds, but founder luck
dominates. Here two sequences compete in one soup, so small per-lineage advantages can
accumulate, and the structure is encoded in the sequence (an adaptation test, not a world comparison).

**Structure** (`SHIELD` in `seeded_worlds.js`, the user's custom-part principle): B's back seeds
ONE convex fan-shaped plate (narrow edge 1 attached to B's back; far edge 4, depth 1). In a
prepared exposure check, a PBBQ chain with two plates has exposure .56–.75 (mean .64) against
.88–1.0 for PAAQ, about a third less. Plates are the field's shadow casters (`walls: [J]`).

**World:** founders PBBQ and PAAQ in 22x22; loose A 12, B 12, P 8, Q 8, J 12;
`energyGate=false` (B's back carries the plate); `pFray` .002; pins; body16. Field `pField` 1e-5,
range 5 (on) or 0 (off). Seeds 1711–1716; 100,000 steps; 12 worlds.

**Outcome:** chain-steps (intact P..Q chains x time, every 500 steps) by sequence (PBBQ versus
PAAQ, founders included), and share S = PBBQ / (PBBQ + PAAQ). **Adaptation reading, fixed now:**
S(field on) > S(field off) in at least 5/6 seeds. Also reported: copies per sequence and plate counts.
The comb (RESULTS 100) predicts S < .5 without the field (structure costs).
