Birth log

Click a square to read its state. The whole chemistry is the compatibility table (which sides may bond, with what probability) and five transitions on one internal state per square: DOCK → REPEL when locally complete or captured, REPEL → TPL on energy, back to DOCK when a unit loses its strand or frays off an end. Copies come out antiparallel, so a child reads as the reverse of its parent.
