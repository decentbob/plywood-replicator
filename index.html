Birth log

Sides are fixed for life: F (face) docks only on a template's F of the same type; L bonds only to a neighbour's R, which is why chains are one-dimensional; K (back) takes only energy. Energy particles are small squares of a third type that never chain: a charged one docks on a back that wants energy, the block re-arms, the particle is left spent and drifts off; it recharges at a background rate, in the sun patch, or at the back of a B block flanked by two As. The whole chemistry is the compatibility table and six transitions on one internal state per square: DOCK → REPEL when locally complete or captured, REPEL → TPL on energy, back to DOCK when a unit loses its strand, frays off an end, or undocks while alone. Copies are antiparallel, so a child reads as the reverse of its parent. In the physics a bond is only a constraint between the two squares it joins.
